Comfort food

Loaded Cheesy Baked Potatoes

Ingredients:

  • 4 large russet potatoes
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 1/4 cup chopped green onions
  • 4 slices of cooked bacon, crumbled
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:

  1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Scrub the potatoes clean and pierce them several times with a fork.
  3. Bake the potatoes directly on the oven rack for about 45-60 minutes, or until tender.
  4. Remove the potatoes from the oven and let them cool slightly.
  5. Cut each potato in half lengthwise and scoop out a portion of the flesh, leaving a small border around the edges.
  6. In a bowl, mix the scooped-out potato flesh with shredded cheddar cheese, sour cream, chopped green onions, crumbled bacon, salt, and pepper.
  7. Spoon the mixture back into the potato skins.
  8. Return the stuffed potatoes to the oven and bake for an additional 15-20 minutes, or until the cheese is melted and bubbly.
  9. Serve hot and enjoy!
