Comfort food

Komodo Chicken

Ingredients:

  • 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup cornstarch
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon paprika
  • 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper (optional, for heat)
  • 2 large eggs, beaten
  • Vegetable oil, for frying

Instructions:

  1. In a large bowl, combine the flour, cornstarch, salt, black pepper, garlic powder, paprika, and cayenne pepper.
  2. Place the chicken pieces in the seasoned flour and coat them evenly.
  3. Beat the eggs in a separate bowl and dip each coated chicken piece into the egg wash.
  4. Heat vegetable oil in a deep fryer or large skillet to 375°F (190°C).
  5. Fry the chicken pieces in batches for 3-4 minutes or until golden brown and cooked through.
  6. Remove from oil and drain on paper towels.
  7. Serve hot with your favorite dipping sauce.
