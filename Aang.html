Comfort food

Mango Sticky Rice

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups glutinous rice
  • 1 can sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 cup coconut milk
  • 1 cup mangoes, sliced

Instructions:

  1. Soak the glutinous rice in water for 30 minutes.
  2. Drain the rice and cook it in a pot with coconut milk until tender.
  3. Mix the sweetened condensed milk into the cooked rice.
  4. Serve the mango sticky rice with sliced mangoes on top.
