Shrimp Scampi With Garlic, Red Pepper Flakes, and Herbs

Ingredients:

  • 1 pound large shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 4 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes
  • 1/4 cup dry white wine
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:

  1. Heat the butter in a large skillet over medium heat.
  2. Add the garlic and red pepper flakes and cook for 1 minute.
  3. Add the shrimp and cook until they turn pink, about 2-3 minutes.
  4. Pour in the wine and lemon juice, and bring to a boil.
  5. Stir in the parsley and season with salt and pepper to taste.
